Artwork & printing requirements

Every order is printed in CMYK. Submit your artwork in the format below so we can go straight to production without back-and-forth.

File format

Vector files only. No raster images (PNG, JPG) on the print area they will not reproduce cleanly at small sizes.

PDF · AI · EPS · SVG

Color mode

All artwork must be submitted in CMYK, not RGB. Colors will be converted automatically if submitted in RGB, and may shift.

CMYK · No spot colors

Bleed & safe zone

Add 0.125 in bleed on all sides. Keep all live content (text, logos) at least 0.0625 in from the trim line.

0.125" bleed · 0.0625" safe

Resolution

If embedding raster elements (photos, textures), minimum 300 DPI at final print size. Logos must remain vector.

300 DPI minimum

Fonts

Outline all fonts before submitting. We cannot guarantee font availability on our end, and missing fonts cause delays.

Outline all text

Distributor ID

By law, every match product must carry the distributor ID. We add "strikematchbooks.com" to the back flap on every order.

Required · Cannot be removed
