Three Brown University rugby players who kept collecting matchbooks and eventually decided to build the company that should have been making them.

We are three college friends who all played rugby at Brown, and we wanted to build something together.

A lot of the matchbooks we collected over the years bring us back to specific memories and moments. The one from the bar in New Orleans. The hotel in the Catskills. The restaurant where someone got engaged. In a world of so much digital, we believe there will be a pushback toward the physical objects that connect us.

"In a world of so much digital, we believe there will be a pushback toward the physical objects that connect us."

What we noticed in this industry is that it's dominated by generic print shops, Etsy sellers, and B2C platforms focused on one-off personal orders. We wanted to go after B2B brands hotels, bars, restaurants, event companies, consumer brands and offer a premium product that can elevate what they put into the world.

The central tension we're trying to hold is fun but premium. Playful enough to feel approachable and rooted in who we are we are not serious or super buttoned up as people but elevated enough to sit clearly above the Etsys and Vistaprints of the world.

We also want the matchbooks themselves to do a lot of the talking. We have orders in production and we're ready to launch. If you're working on something that deserves a match, we'd love to hear about it.
