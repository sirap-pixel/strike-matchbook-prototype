About Strike

Three college friends. A lot of matchbooks. One big idea.

Strike was built by three friends who played rugby together at Brown. Over the years, we kept collecting matchbooks from restaurants, bars, hotels the kind of thing you pocket on the way out and find months later, and it takes you right back.

A lot of the matchbooks we collected bring us back to specific memories and moments. In a world of so much digital, we believe there will be a pushback toward physical objects that connect us. We noticed the industry was dominated by generic print shops, Etsy sellers, and one-off B2C platforms. We wanted to go after B2B brands hotels, bars, restaurants, event companies and offer something premium that could elevate what they put into the world.

The tension we're holding: fun but premium. Playful enough to feel like us, elevated enough to sit clearly above the Etsys and Vistaprints of the world. We have orders in production and we're ready to launch.
